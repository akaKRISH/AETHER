# Cost management

The native fixture has zero paid spend and does not need provider keys. Later proposed local defaults from v0.2 are one worker, two CPU threads, 2 GiB worker memory, a 300 s execution timeout, 200 MiB output cap, 30 min campaign wall cap, 20 attempts, 60,000 model tokens and zero paid credits. Native fixture usage and study caps are separate. Treat these as design limits pending calibration.

Use integer quantities, separate units and explicit reservations. Record estimates, holds and actuals. Late provider reports that exceed reservation stop scheduling and trigger a deviation record. Free credits depend on availability. Paid spend or data transfers require explicit prior owner approval. [ECAR policy](../research/ECAR_SPECIFICATION.md)

# Mathematics for AETHER

Learn ratios, uncertainty and constrained optimization in sequence. A ratio needs a valid denominator. A normalized score requires declared bounds and direction. Two independent sources can disagree, so preserve evidence and define how the protocol handles that disagreement. Paired study differences compare variants on the same task/seed block, with task-level uncertainty reflecting clustered repeats.

Exercise: with 3/4 and 7/10 accuracy, compute the individual rates and the pooled rate. Explain why averaging two percentages answers a different question than pooling observations. Then explain why an unresolved claim with high estimated gain cannot bypass a hard resource cap. [ECAR math](../research/MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATIONS.md)

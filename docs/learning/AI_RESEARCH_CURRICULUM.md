# AI research curriculum

Start by distinguishing candidate, metric, claim and evaluator. Recreate a fixed baseline from documented data. Learn how a training/dev/test separation prevents parameter choice using final labels. Next learn controlled comparison and ablation: change one research factor while keeping task, evaluator and available budget fixed. Finally study paired estimation, uncertainty and negative findings.

Exercises progress from the synthetic fixture to a reviewed CPU baseline and then an ECAR paper reading. For each reading, record the hypothesis, comparator, experimental unit, evidence, cost and one alternative explanation. [Research hypotheses](../research/RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md)

# Internship preparation

Use the employer’s official role description to record the program cycle, job ID, date checked, location, degree and enrollment constraints, availability, prior experience and published work authorization details. Review timing against coursework and exams. Where a criterion is absent, seek the organization’s applicable clarification before drawing an eligibility conclusion. [Career alignment and role snapshots](CAREER_ALIGNMENT.md)

For a systems role, prepare a testable story about a broken invariant, the reason for selecting the fix, recovery behavior and measured result. For a research role, prepare the hypothesis, baseline, control, failure evidence and uncertainty. For each story bring one artifact and explain what you personally built, reviewed or reproduced. Use measured numbers only.

Practice questions include explaining who owns synthetic labels in Rust, why the evaluator owns verdicts, how a stale completion can double-account a budget and how randomization changes the comparison. Walk one example by hand, then tie it to the project check. Include the open limitation and the experiment or test that could address it.

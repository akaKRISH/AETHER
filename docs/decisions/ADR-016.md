# ADR-016 · Evidence-grounded hypothesis discovery

Status: Authorized by the owner for APO-03 from `bed6758`.

Use deterministic Rust rules over a persisted graph slice at depth two. The slice holds up to 64 nodes and 192 links. The engine returns up to 12 ranked proposals with stable IDs, the graph digest, and up to six exact references per role. A proposal can cite an archived artifact by digest and JSON pointer or cite passages and captured campaign records through their existing provenance. Generated records have unverified status. The read path has no access to execution, approval or verification actions.

Exact matched comparison scopes can motivate a contradiction check. The engine requires opposite paired signs and different provenance groups. An asserted contradiction with unknown scope instead counts as unqualified. Operator-annotated comparisons require a link to a pinned source claim or evidence revision and carry maximum uncertainty. Identical source text under different groups cannot produce an annotated conflict. Boundary rules compare aggregate strict win rates by profile within the same task, size, metric, methods and budget. A difference of at least ten percentage points motivates a prospective test. The negative-result rule looks for a majority of nonwins. Groups preserve ties and losses separately, and identical archived comparison records within a source group are counted once.

Evidence gaps motivate operational clarity reviews. Composition requires two grounded methods linked to the same anchor. Transfer requires a shared grounded method and different declared dataset domains. These proposals require controlled future tests. Associations and incomplete local coverage supply no causal or novelty conclusion.

Ranking uses `3 × testability + 3 × evidence quality + 2 × utility − 2 × uncertainty − estimated cost`. The design rubric supplies testability, utility and initial uncertainty on a 0–4 scale. Evidence quality grades provenance. Operator assertions and bibliographic metadata alone score zero. Campaign observations score one, exact passages and archived comparisons score two, and qualified independent receipts score three. Two provenance groups can add a point after exact passage copies are removed. Group diversity does not establish independent outcomes. An incomplete slice or lower grade raises uncertainty up to four. Estimated cost uses the checked number of proposed evaluations or reviews. Up to 12, 64 and 320 units score one, two and three respectively. Larger evaluations score four. Ties sort by ID.

The existing graph remains the persistent input. Read-only generation permits repeatable replay against the same revision set. The model-assisted alternative stays deferred because these rules cover the bounded pilot and expose their assumptions. [APOLLO plan](../research/APOLLO_EXECUTION_PLAN.md)

# ADR-008 · Apache-2.0 candidate

Status: Proposed and unadopted

## Context

The intended project should be usable by researchers and contributors without silently relicensing assets.

## Decision

Propose Apache-2.0 for scoped first-party work, subject to owner approval and asset rights review. Select release terms per asset class.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

MIT and BSD-3-Clause provide shorter permissive terms. Apache-2.0 adds an express patent grant and notice obligations. Defer adoption where rights or distribution scope remain unresolved.

## Verification and review trigger

Record provenance, licence and required notices for each distributed asset, and confirm owner permission before adding a final license.

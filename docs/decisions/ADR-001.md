# ADR-001 · CLI-first modular monolith

Status: Proposed

## Context

One local researcher needs a debuggable system before shared service demand is demonstrated.

## Decision

Put the synchronous domain and orchestration behind a native CLI. Define inward dependencies and add adapters as they acquire active behavior.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

An HTTP-first service adds authentication and operational state. A single undifferentiated crate makes domain and I/O boundaries harder to inspect. The chosen structure supports one process with distinct component responsibilities.

## Verification and review trigger

Test all user-visible paths through CLI and verify that the core can be tested without I/O. Revisit after evidence of multi-user demand or a measured bottleneck.

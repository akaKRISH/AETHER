# ADR-015 · Persistent scientific knowledge graph

Status: Authorized by the owner for APO-02 from `3bac54c`.

Store the graph in the existing literature SQLite database. Metadata and text imports project graph revisions in the same transaction as their source records. For existing archive data, `graph-paper` backfills one work. `graph-campaign` captures a snapshot from the trusted campaign store after checking the frozen protocol bytes. It writes a research record in the graph. The existing campaign rules retain authority over claims, verification and approval.

Typed identities combine entity kind with a namespace and a stable key. Catalog papers use the normalized canonical work key. Source passages use the work, document digest and exact byte range. Operator records use explicitly assigned keys. Reimporting a previously seen observation preserves its identity and history. Material changes append revisions. Edges record both endpoint revisions, and SQLite enforces those references. Citation observations whose targets are absent preserve the original identifiers until their aliases enter the catalog. Citation cycles are valid. Claim and hypothesis dependency cycles roll back the enclosing batch.

Metadata and citation assertions are reported. Automatically projected passages are extracted. An operator may explicitly classify a pinned source span as a reported claim. Operator records are proposed and their relationship assertions are inferred. A verification receipt earns the independently verified status at capture only through a trusted campaign snapshot with a verified claim, matching protocol and evaluator, and a passed replay. Current authority remains in the campaign store. Conflicting links and all captured revisions are retained. Confidence in basis points is supplied by the operator and carries no verdict weight.

The graph uses indexed entity lookup, adjacency, history, source groups and exact passage hashes. Groups expose shared provenance. Matching hashes detect byte-identical passages across source groups. Neither establishes statistical independence or semantic equivalence. Select a root and traverse up to three hops, 64 nodes and 192 edges. Truncation is explicit. Exports include root history, the endpoint revisions needed to interpret all returned links, and the SHA-256 of the compact UTF-8 serialization of the graph body.

An external graph database would add a service and a second transaction boundary. Revisit only after representative workloads show a limit of the indexed local design. The [APOLLO plan](../research/APOLLO_EXECUTION_PLAN.md) records the operator workflow and validation.

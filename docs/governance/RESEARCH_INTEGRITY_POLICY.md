# Research integrity policy

Define a falsifiable question and obtain owner and appropriate research review before freezing protocol. Record task set and split digests, evaluator, hypothesis and prioritized comparisons, model versions where applicable, paired seeds, budget, exclusion rules, analysis plan and expected deviations. Keep development and held-out tasks separate. Record issues before interpreting results and retain all eligible attempts, failures and negative findings.

Trusted evaluator outputs, independent replay and immutable provenance govern verdicts. If an assumption fails or an artifact changes, append a correction that names the evidence and affected claims. A protocol correction after freeze creates a new version and an explicit decision about which runs can be analyzed together. Missing evidence leads to an inconclusive or rejected verdict as the protocol directs. Avoid post-hoc tuning on held-out tasks.

Report denominators, uncertainty, deviations, workload limits, asset rights and AI assistance where relevant. Distinguish research design and synthetic examples from observations. Establish authorship and have authors verify the artifact bundle before any submission. The owner approves disclosure and publication after rights, security and independent review.

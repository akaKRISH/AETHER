# Research publication roadmap

| Stage | Evidence to produce | Exit review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Scope, closest prior art, research question, endpoint and falsification conditions | Owner accepts a bounded contribution and unresolved risks are recorded |
| Native fixture | Local reproducible classification metric and evidence trail | A second run agrees with the registered predicate and integrity failures remain visible |
| Pilot | Six development tasks, complete trace, timing/cost envelope and plausible sample size | Owner reviews feasibility, hardware constraints and leakage controls |
| Preregistration | Immutable protocol and analysis with one primary contrast | Timestamp the freeze before held-out access. Obtain independent review where available. |
| Main study | Frozen matrix, complete outcomes, uncertainty, limitations and deviations | Independent reproduction of at least one complete study run plus review of all evidence classes |
| Release candidate | Reproducible package, contribution framing, license and venue disclosures | Author, data rights, security and owner release approvals are recorded |

The first paper-sized question is the empirical value of dependency-aware allocation under the studied caps. An engineering report can stand on a usable reproducibility artifact and well-measured null result. Claims of methodological originality need the full review described in [Literature review](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). Intended venue, authors, mentor and public registration platform remain owner decisions.

# Research references

The [comparison matrix](COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md) and [related work review](RELATED_WORK.md) provide primary links for the full screened set. This reading list prioritizes the study's closest method, evidence and reproducibility questions. Dates identify publication or the documented version, with 2026 preprints still subject to revision.

- Guo et al. (2026-09-30), CAVEAT. Claim dependencies, correlated verification errors and adaptive verification under cost. [Research Square](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11035327/v1).
- Ning et al. (2026-09-28, v2), Discovery Certification Protocol. Frozen records, deterministic verdicts and matched information controls. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09219v2).
- Wen and Liu (2026-11-16, v2), Autonomous Science Bench. Budgeted experiment campaigns and multiple success gates. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18449v2).
- Wang et al. (ICML 2026), FIRE-Bench. Claim-centric evaluation for rediscovery. [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26d.html).
- Lange et al. (2026-10-13), The Agentic Scientist. Evidence and human oversight in a scripted-evaluation research harness. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933).
- Starace et al. (2025-04-02), PaperBench. Hierarchical evaluation for scientific replication artifacts. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/paperbench/).
- Romera-Paredes et al. (2023-12-14 online), FunSearch. Objective-based evolutionary search over functions. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6).
- Novikov et al. (2025-06-16), AlphaEvolve. Evolutionary improvement of evaluator-scored algorithms. [Technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131) · [2026-05-07 deployment cases](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/).
- Ding et al. (2026 preprint), Autonomous Research Agents survey. Evidence artifacts in its screened research corpus. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05179).
- Leo et al. (2024), Recording provenance of workflow runs with RO-Crate. [PLOS ONE](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309210).
- W3C (2013-04-30), PROV-O. Interchange vocabulary for provenance. [Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/).
- Anthropic Engineering (2026-04-08), Managed Agents. Separate interfaces for durable session progress, model harness and sandbox. [Engineering write-up](https://www.anthropic.com/research/managed-agents).

To cite an external benchmark result, identify the model, harness, environment, dataset version, metrics and dates in that source. Document differences before comparing it with AETHER. To cite an AETHER result, wait for an approved frozen study and identify its protocol and result digests.

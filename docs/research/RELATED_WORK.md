# Closest related work

| Work and status | Confirmed overlap | AETHER interpretation and test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAVEAT, Guo et al., 2026-09-30 Research Square preprint. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11035327/v1) | Builds claim dependency graphs from multi-agent traces and adaptively allocates verification via cost-adjusted information gain. Surfaces effects of correlated verifier errors. | This is the closest scheduling analogue. ECAR additionally chooses candidate exploration and creates fresh experiment evidence within one campaign budget. Test those choices with a trusted executable verifier and consider a cost-aware prioritization comparator. Scope the applicability of their posterior assumptions to independent measured artifacts. |
| Discovery Certification Protocol, Ning et al., 2026-09-07 preprint, revised 2026-09-28. [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09219v2) | Audits useful improvement, access to source information, experiment contribution and reproducible decisions using frozen records and matched controls. | This is the closest verdict analogue. A good score can arise without performing discovery. Add matched controls for information availability and evidence provenance where the claim implies discovery from new measurements. |
| The Agentic Scientist, Lange et al., 2026-10-13 preprint. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933) · [Project](https://pub.sakana.ai/agentic-scientist/) | Wraps coding agents with scripted evaluation, documented evidence, rubric review and human-guided ML research. | Research orchestration, evidence generation and evaluator review have concrete precedent. Test independent replay constraints and measurable allocation effects as distinct research variables. |
| Autonomous Science Bench, Wen and Liu, 2026-10-13 preprint, revised 2026-11-16. [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18449v2) | Budgeted interactive experiment selection and dual objective gates under a published lab interface. | Benchmark budget-aware exploration is established. AETHER should distinguish gains from allocation to new experiments from gains due to independent replay and challenge. |
| FIRE-Bench, Wang et al., ICML 2026. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26d.html) | Claim-level evaluation after agents try to rediscover established scientific insights. | Use tighter task predicates where available. Compare scientific coverage and direction of errors, including incomplete attempts and unsupported claims. |
| ACED-Bench, Chen et al., 2026-10-09 preprint, revised 2026-11-08. [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14381v2) | Hidden causal worlds evaluate choices under experiment limits, plus evidence retention and eventual decisions. | Include synthetic tasks with pivotal evidence and a counterfactual oracle when the pilot has a suitable CPU-only design. |
| Active Causal Discovery Benchmark, Deb et al., 2026-09-16 preprint. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31675) | Evaluates identification of causal graph structure under limited interventions. | Treat cost-constrained information acquisition as established. AETHER claim prerequisites express evidence eligibility and do not encode inferred causal graph structure. |
| Rethinking Verification in Agent Harnesses, Zhang et al., 2026-10-25 preprint. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34577) | Assesses when independent review and other checks improve outcomes under test-time cost. | Separate scheduling of a check from its authority. An independent critic can suggest a challenge, while the trusted evaluator measures its result. |
| When Stale Constraints Go Unchecked, Nakayashiki, 2026-08-26 preprint, revised 2026-08-28. [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25553v3) | Explores budgeted provenance inspection, scoped critical-path design and the effect of superseded memory records. | Verify that ECAR prioritizes stale or pivotal evidence under actual held-out uncertainty. Keep provenance immutable while allowing current verdicts to change. |

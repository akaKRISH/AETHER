# Product vision

A researcher should be able to ask which conditions a result actually survived, follow links to the measurements, and recreate the evaluation without relying on the original author's narrative. AETHER aims to make that workflow practical for small, bounded computational tasks.

Three outcomes guide design. The first is a repeatable local baseline with clear failure messages. The second is a fair study of how evidence dependencies affect verification allocation. The third is a maintained extension surface for reviewed task packs where demand and safety justify it. A general chatbot, autonomous publication and self-modifying control plane are outside the charter.

A policy that fails to improve verified utility still produces a useful result if it was evaluated under a frozen, fair protocol. Measure learning and reproducibility as outcomes in their own right. [Success metrics](SUCCESS_METRICS.md)
